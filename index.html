Sharkie

Key Descriptions

  • W A S D Move
  • E Fin Slap

Description

Sharkie wants to fight the Army of the Seas and has to clear his own way through the ocean.

Attacks

  • Fin Slap Effective against the puffer fish
  • Bubble Attack Effective against the jellyfish

Bubbles

  • Normal Bubble Your standard shot
  • Poison Bubble The only bubble that hurts the endboss

Shop

With 10 coins Sharkie can either refill his health to 100% or buy poison bottles to shoot poison bubbles again.
